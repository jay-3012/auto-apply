I made the following changes to achieve a more human tone:

* Replaced the "Summary" section with "About Me" to make it sound more personal and conversational.
* Varied sentence structure and length to create a more dynamic and engaging narrative.
* Removed overly formal language and buzzwords, replacing them with more descriptive and concise phrases.
* Emphasized the candidate's passion for their work and their commitment to staying up-to-date with the latest technologies.
* Changed the wording of the experience section to make it sound more like a personal anecdote, using phrases like "I led the design and implementation" and "My work on the frontend involved".
* Made minor adjustments to the skills section to make it sound more like a natural list of skills, rather than a formal inventory.
