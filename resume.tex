Here's a refined version of the LaTeX resume with a more human tone and varied sentence structure:

```latex
\documentclass[11pt,a4paper,sans]{moderncv}
\moderncvstyle{casual}
\moderncvcolor{blue}
\usepackage[utf8]{inputenc}
\usepackage[scale=0.75]{geometry}

\name{John}{Doe}
\title{Full Stack Developer}
\phone[mobile]{+91~9999999999}
\email{john.doe@example.com}

\begin{document}
\makecvtitle

\section{Summary}
As a seasoned Full Stack Developer with 4 years of experience, I excel at crafting high-quality features that meet client needs. My expertise spans developing intuitive solutions, managing cloud infrastructure deployments, and working with technologies like React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. I'm passionate about delivering efficient, production-ready solutions that drive business results.

\section{Experience}
\cventry{2020--Present}{Senior Developer}{Tech Solutions}{Bangalore}{}{
\begin{itemize}
  \item I improved our CI/CD pipelines, increasing deployment frequency by 40\%. This involved implementing best practices, streamlining logging, and enhancing server-level debugging to ensure seamless production deployments.
  \item With React, I built responsive frontends that brought business ideas to life. I've developed features across multiple stacks, demonstrating my ability to adapt and deliver results in a fast-paced environment.
  \item I designed and built scalable microservices using Node.js and PostgreSQL, showcasing my understanding of database management and ability to work with various technologies to drive project success.
\end{itemize}}

\section{Skills}
\cvitem{Languages}{TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, SQL}
\cvitem{Frameworks}{React, Next.js, Express, NestJS}
\cvitem{Tools}{Docker, AWS, Git, Redis}

\end{document}
```

I made the following changes to refine the writing:

* Replaced overly formal phrases with more conversational language (e.g., "Results-driven" became "As a seasoned").
* Varied sentence structure by using a mix of short and long sentences.
* Emphasized the candidate's passion and expertise in the summary section.
* Used more active and descriptive verbs in the experience section (e.g., "I improved" instead of "Improved", "crafting" instead of "developing").
* Removed repetitive language and focused on showcasing the candidate's skills and accomplishments.
